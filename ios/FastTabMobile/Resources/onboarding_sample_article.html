You opened it with every intention of reading it. That was three weeks ago. The tab is still there, squeezed between a dashboard and a document, its title cut down to a favicon and two letters.

Most of us keep tabs the way we keep a pile of magazines by the bed: not because we will read them tonight, but because closing them feels like giving up. FastTab for iPhone is built for the moment you finally have a few quiet minutes and your Mac is nowhere near you.

Your tabs, somewhere you can read them

FastTab syncs the tabs open on your Mac to your iPhone over your own iCloud account. Nothing passes through anyone else's server. Open the Tabs tab and the article you left on your desk is right there.

Tap Open in Reader and FastTab strips the page down to what you came for: the words, the images that belong to them, and a comfortable column of text. You are reading in Reader right now.

A tab is a promise to your future self. Reader is how you keep it.

Keep what matters

Try it on this paragraph: long-press any text, drag to choose a passage, then pick a highlight color. Highlights are saved with the article, and every one you make is collected under Highlights, so the one good idea in a long piece is never lost in the scroll.

Close the reader halfway through and FastTab remembers where you stopped. The next time you open the article, it picks up at the same line.

Things worth coming back to

Some tabs are not forgotten, just early. The Read tab brings back bookmarks you have not opened in a month and tabs that have sat idle for days, so an old favourite gets a second chance. Shuffle deals out random cards from everything you have saved when you want to be surprised.

And when you find something on your iPhone that deserves a bigger screen, share it to FastTab and choose Send to Mac. Your Mac opens it right away, or the moment it wakes up.

That is the whole idea: fewer promises left open, more of them kept. Close this article when you are ready, and happy reading.
