# App Review notes — Fast Tab for iPhone 1.0.0 (build 11) — DRAFT

**Where to find the in-app purchase**
- Open the **More** tab → **Premium** card (above Account). It shows the price from the App Store, **Subscribe / Start Free Trial**, **Restore Purchases** and **Manage Subscription**.
- The **last onboarding screen** ("Fast Tab is free") also explains Premium and offers **Start free trial** or **Maybe later**.
- Tapping **Summary** in the reader menu, or turning on **Natural voice** in Read Aloud settings, opens the same subscription screen.

**What is sold**
- One auto-renewing subscription group, "Natural Voice + AI Summary": monthly (fasttab.naturalVoice.monthly, $4.99) and yearly (fasttab.naturalVoice.yearly, $39.99), both with a **7-day free trial** for new subscribers. No other in-app purchases.
- Premium unlocks **Natural Voice** (cloud voices, languages other than English) and **AI Summary**. Everything else (reading, highlights, tab sync, built-in voices) is free and never gated.

**Testing the purchase**
- Use a **sandbox Apple ID**. Purchases are linked to the user's account, so **Sign in with Apple is required before buying** (the paywall shows the Sign in with Apple button first). Browsing and reading need no sign-in.
- **Restore Purchases:** More → Premium, or at the bottom of the subscription screen. **Manage / cancel:** More → Premium → Manage Subscription (opens Apple's subscription sheet).

**Background audio**
- The `audio` background mode is used only for **Read Aloud** (listening to an article with the screen locked).
