What's new
- Reader: new Headings font option in Reading Settings. Fraunces, a bold serif, works with Vietnamese too.
- Your Mac's status (More > Connected Macs) now appears within seconds on a fresh install.
- AI Summary in the reader's … menu, with a prompt you can edit. Summary text follows your reader text size and font.
- Find in Article: search inside the article you're reading.
- Highlights is now a tab with a two-column card gallery and its own Shuffle. Shuffle on Read moved to a top-left button.
- Tab list shows site icons from your Mac instead of a globe.
- Delete a whole bookmark folder at once.
- Faster typing in comments.

What to test
- Reading Settings > Headings: switch to Fraunces and back; try an English and a Vietnamese article.
- Delete the app, reinstall, then open More > Connected Macs: your Mac and its tabs should show within about 30 seconds.
- Open an article, … menu > Summary; edit the prompt and summarise again.
- … menu > Find in Article: search a word, step through the matches.
- Highlights tab: browse the cards, open one, try Shuffle.
- Everyday use: reading, highlighting, closing Mac tabs from the phone.
