What's new
- New English voice (beta) for Read Aloud: English articles are now read with a natural-sounding voice that runs entirely on your iPhone. Free, no subscription, works offline. Choose from four voices: Heart and Puck (American), Isabella and Fable (British). Other languages work as before.
- Tabs: "Add to FastTab List" on any tab; every save asks whether to also close the tab.
- The Read tab's "View all" opens a full screen with FastTab List, Bookmarks, Read and Trash. Links you've finished reading move to Read.
- The card-style tab switcher is gone; the swipeable tab list is the one tab view.
- Transcript clean-up is now opt-in, under More > Experimental.
- Articles with diagrams keep them; returning to an open article no longer freezes briefly.
- Pull to refresh no longer cancels itself or shows an error.

What to test
- Open an English article and press Play. It should speak with the new voice right away and highlight each sentence as it goes.
- Try all four voices: ••• > Reading Settings > Read Aloud. Is any voice clearly better or worse?
- Listen to a long article with the screen locked and with AirPods. Any gaps, stutters or stops?
- Try airplane mode: the English voice should keep working.
- Try the speed slider at slow and fast settings.
- Listen for mispronounced words, especially names, numbers and dates. Tell us which ones.
- Open a non-English article: Read Aloud should work exactly as before.
- Turn off "English voice (beta)" in Read Aloud settings: English articles go back to the built-in iPhone voice.
- Tell us anything that feels slow, confusing or broken.
